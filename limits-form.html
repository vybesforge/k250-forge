Build a limits.json. The engine clamps every write to max power here, in code — a driver cannot exceed it by typo, drift, or persuasion.
USE AT YOUR OWN RISK. The K250-4S is a high-power e-stim device and its own manual says so. This page makes it controllable, not safe. Start low and build. Percentages do not transfer between pad placements — outer skin on limbs is far less sensitive than far more sensitive skin, so 38% on a forearm and 38% on a far more sensitive site are neither the same sensation nor the same risk. Decide ceilings calm, before a scene; never renegotiate mid-scene.

Hardware kill switch: hold any knob on the box for 2 seconds and it powers off. Give that to whoever is in the room. It needs no software, no network, and no AI.
