K250 — set your limits

Three controls. Decide them now, while you're calm, and they get enforced in code.
At your own risk. High-power e-stim device — this page makes it controllable, not safe. Start low and build. Percentages don't transfer between pad placements: outer skin is far less sensitive than far more sensitive skin, so the same figure is a different sensation and a different risk. Read the hard stops in Session & safety below before you use any of this.

2 · Session & safety

30 min

Enforced, not advisory: the tools keep a session ledger and refuse to start once the budget is used. Up to 240 minutes. A fresh session begins after 15 quiet minutes, or with --reset-session.

The hard stops — every session, no exceptions
  • Never a pad path across the chest or through the heart.
  • Never on broken, irritated or numb skin — or anywhere they can't feel it.
  • Never while plugged into mains power.
  • Never on someone who can't speak or signal, or who can't be seen by whoever is in the room.
  • Keep loops and pads loose — circulation at the electrode is the one thing here that can cause real injury.
  • If it burns rather than stings, or the skin doesn't settle back to normal pink, stop — and don't use that site again that night.
  • If they feel nothing, turn the power down before you touch anything else, then check the loop: pads properly seated, cable attached, connection complete. A pad that's loose or half-attached concentrates the current into a smaller area — and that is what burns people. Feeling nothing is a fault, not an invitation to push harder.
Hardware kill switch — hold any knob on the box for 2 seconds and it powers off. Give that to whoever is in the room. It works with no software and no network.
