K250 — set your limits

Three controls. Decide them now, while you're calm, and they get enforced in code.
At your own risk. The K250-4S is a high-power e-stim device — its own manual says so. This page makes it controllable, not safe. Start low and build up slowly. Numbers don't transfer between pad placements: outer skin on arms or torso is far less sensitive than far more sensitive skin, so the same percentage is a different sensation — and a different risk — somewhere else.

Session & safety

Never while plugged into mains power — the manual's own rule.
Loops and pads kept loose. Circulation at the electrode is the one thing here that can cause real injury.
No pad path across the chest or through the heart.
Check the skin under the pads after every session.
Whoever's wearing it must be able to speak or signal. If they can't, hold the ceiling and go longer, not harder.
Hardware kill switch — hold any knob on the box for 2 seconds and it powers off. Give that to whoever is in the room. It works with no software and no network.
Save it as limits.json next to the tools, then run k250-scene speed_sweep --base 5 --secs 60 — the engine clamps to it automatically.
